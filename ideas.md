# Navkar Tubes & Tools — Website Design Brief

## Reference
The reference site is https://navkar-one.vercel.app/ — a professional industrial pipes dealer website for Navkar Tubes & Tools, an authorized Jindal pipes dealer in Ahmedabad since 1995. The design is faithful to this reference.

## Chosen Approach: Industrial Authority

This is a direct replication of the reference website. The design philosophy follows an "Industrial Authority" aesthetic — combining the gravitas of a three-decade-old industrial business with modern web design.

### Design Movement
Industrial Editorial — inspired by heavy industry marketing (steel, construction, engineering) with clean editorial typography and structured information hierarchy.

### Core Principles
1. **Gravitas & Trust** — The design communicates three decades of industrial credibility through weight, contrast, and structured layouts
2. **Information Density with Clarity** — Technical specifications, product ranges, and certifications are presented with precision and readability
3. **Dark Teal Authority** — The primary color system uses deep teal/navy as the brand anchor, conveying stability and professionalism
4. **Industrial Imagery** — Photography of pipes, steel, and facilities grounds the digital experience in physical reality

### Color Philosophy
- **Primary Dark Teal**: #1B2E3B (deep navy-teal) — used for headers, nav, hero overlays, dark sections
- **Brand Teal**: #2D7A82 — used for CTAs, accents, highlights, buttons
- **Background Light**: #F5F5F5 / white — used for content sections
- **Text Dark**: #1a1a1a — body text on light backgrounds
- **Text White**: #ffffff — text on dark backgrounds
- **Gold/Amber Accent**: #C4A44E — used sparingly for labels and decorative elements
- **Muted Gray**: #6B7280 — secondary text, captions

### Layout Paradigm
- Full-viewport hero with centered content and background imagery
- Marquee/ticker for continuous brand messaging
- Asymmetric image grids for the about section
- Card-based grids for features and products
- Numbered service blocks for capabilities
- Full-width dark sections for contrast breaks

### Signature Elements
1. **Marquee ticker** — continuous scrolling text banner with key brand claims
2. **Bracketed section labels** — `[ PROVEN ADVANTAGE ]`, `[ TECHNICAL INSPECTION DASHBOARD ]` style labels
3. **Large numerical stat** — animated counter for years of experience

### Interaction Philosophy
- Smooth scroll-triggered animations (fade-in, slide-up)
- Tab-based technical dashboard
- Interactive pipe weight calculator
- Accordion FAQ section
- Hover effects on cards with subtle transforms

### Animation
- Staggered fade-in on scroll for sections
- Counter animation for the years-of-experience number
- Smooth tab transitions in the technical dashboard
- Gentle hover lift on cards

### Typography System
- **Headings**: Serif font (Playfair Display or similar) for large display text
- **Body**: Clean sans-serif (Inter or similar) for readable content
- **Labels/Tags**: Uppercase tracking-wide for section labels
- **Technical values**: Large bold numbers for specifications

### Brand Essence
Professional, authoritative, and deeply rooted in industrial expertise — serving procurement teams who need reliable steel pipe supply with full documentation and three decades of proven track record.

### Brand Voice
- Headlines: Direct, confident, industry-specific
- CTAs: Action-oriented and professional ("Explore Products", "Request a Quote")
- Microcopy: Technical and precise

### Signature Brand Color
**Deep Teal (#1B2E3B)** — the unmistakable dark navy-teal that defines the Navkar brand identity across all touchpoints.

## Style Decisions
- Use DM Serif Display for display headings and Space Grotesk for body text
- Bracketed section labels are mandatory signature typography: every major section eyebrow should use the `[ UPPERCASE TECHNICAL LABEL ]` format with wide tracking
- Deep Teal #0A1628 must dominate all authority/proof sections, while Brand Teal #2D7A82 is reserved for headline emphasis, icons, active states, and key technical highlights
- Copy voice must remain procurement-specific and proof-led, favoring phrases about Jindal authorization, MTC documentation, OD ranges, dispatch yards, stock availability, and inspection
- Use the original Navkar logo and images from the reference site plus custom-generated hero/product photography
